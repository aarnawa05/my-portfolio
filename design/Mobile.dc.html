PintOS

The scheduler starved high-priority threads whenever a low-priority thread held a lock they needed. I implemented priority donation — a blocked thread lends its priority through the lock holder, recursively across chained locks — plus syscalls for user programs and a timer that blocks sleepers instead of busy-waiting.
