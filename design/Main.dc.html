PintOS
C · Kernel · Concurrency
{{c0}}
The scheduler starved high-priority threads whenever a low-priority thread held a lock they needed. I implemented priority donation — a blocked thread lends its priority through the lock holder, recursively across chained locks — added the syscalls that let user programs talk to the kernel, and rewrote the timer to block sleeping threads instead of busy-waiting.

Memory Allocator
85% util · 1,750 req/s
{{c1}}
A malloc and free that manage their own heap: explicit free lists threaded through the unused blocks, boundary-tag coalescing so neighboring frees merge instead of fragmenting, 16-byte alignment throughout. A heap consistency checker catches corruption, misalignment, and invalid frees before they become mystery crashes. It averages 85% utilization at 1,750 requests per second.
